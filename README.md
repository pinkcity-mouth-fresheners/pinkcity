# PinkCity Mouth Freshener

**Experience the authentic taste of Jaipur tradition since 1982.**

PinkCity Mouth Freshener is a premium manufacturer and supplier of traditional Indian mukhwas, paan, and mouth fresheners. Rooted in the rich cultural heritage of Jaipur, Rajasthan, we have been crafting high-quality, authentic blends that celebrate the timeless Indian tradition of hospitality for over four decades.

## 🌟 About the Brand

Established in 1982, **PinkCity Mouth Freshener** has grown from a local initiative into a trusted household name across India. Our journey is built on a passion for purity and a commitment to delivering the true essence of Rajasthani flavors.

Every pinch of our mukhwas tells a story of authentic Jaipur taste, combined with modern hygiene standards and uncompromising quality.

## 🍃 Core Offerings

We specialize in a massive variety of mouth freshening products, meticulously curated for every palate:

- **Paan Mukhwas**: Authentic paan experiences available both with and without supari (including gold-standard variants).
- **Signature Mukhwas**: A wide range of mixes from traditional Marwari and Punjabi blends to premium segments like _Kashmiri Gold_ and _Royal Bambaiya_.
- **Saunf Products**: Perfectly roasted and aromatic fennel seeds, including specialties like _Rajasthani Roasted Saunf_ and _Green Madrasi Saunf_.
- **Dry Date Products**: Innovative blends like _Gulab Khajur_ and _Herbal Khajur Chura_.
- **Supari Specialties**: A vast collection of sweet, plain, and silver-coated supari products.
- **Confectionery & Others**: Healthy seeds and sweet treats like _Flax Seeds_ and _Kesar Mishri_.
